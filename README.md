Bring satellite imagery, field surveys, geographic data and operational records into one decision-making platform.
- Monitor infrastructure, land use, agricultural activity and project coverage.
- Combine GIS layers with field reports and operational datasets.
- Identify changes, anomalies and underserved locations.
- Generate evidence-backed reports for management, investors or donors.
